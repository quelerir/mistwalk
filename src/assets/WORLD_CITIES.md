# World cities with regions

`world-cities.json` lists 7266 Natural Earth populated places (public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) with
their region. Generated by `scripts/build-world-cities.sh` (needs network and node; mapshaper runs through npx);
do not edit by hand. Cache the downloads with `WORLD_CITIES_CACHE=<dir>`.

Shape: `{ regions: { <adm1_code>: { ru, en } }, cities: [ { ru, en, w (Wikidata id or null), c (ISO country),
r (region key or null), p (population), la, lo } ] }`. About 970 KB on disk.

- The region of a city is found at build time by point in polygon against Natural Earth `admin_1_states_provinces`;
  the polygons are not shipped. The key is `adm1_code`, not the ISO 3166-2 code: Natural Earth has Moscow and Moscow
  Oblast with the codes swapped, and a few other codes shared by several areas.
- 18 places fall in no polygon (`r: null`); places in countries the app does not know (`src/lib/geo/countries.ts`) and
  a repeated Wikidata id within a country are dropped (80 rows).
- Cleanup in the compaction step: 5 unlabelled twins of a real city are dropped (a second "Натал" in Amazonas); a place
  whose region lies in another country loses its region (Mukusso); two regions of a country with the same name are told
  apart by their biggest city (the Altai Krai is labelled "Республика Алтай" by Natural Earth).
- A place inside one of the five republic borders (`republics.json`) gets the republic's code as its country
  (`XA`, `XS`, `XT`, `XN`, `XL`), and so do places Natural Earth labels `CYN` or `SOL`, which have no ISO code (Kyrenia
  sits just outside the coarse border). Sukhumi and Tskhinvali are therefore not listed under Georgia.
- Borders, country and region membership are Natural Earth's, including for disputed areas. A city near a border can land in the neighbour (Podolsk is placed in the "Москва" polygon).
