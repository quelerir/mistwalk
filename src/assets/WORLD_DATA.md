# World border data

`world.geojson` (country polygons, property `code` = ISO 3166-1 alpha-2) and `world-points.geojson` (one inner point
per country) are generated by `scripts/build-world-geojson.sh` from Natural Earth 1:50m `admin_0_countries`
(public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/). Do not edit them by hand; rerun the script
(`scripts/build-world-geojson.sh [simplify%]`, needs network and node; mapshaper runs through npx).

- Code comes from `ISO_A2_EH` (plain `ISO_A2` is `-99` for France and Norway); features with `-99` are dropped.
- Simplified to 40 % with `keep-shapes`: `world.geojson` is about 620 KB, `world-points.geojson` about 25 KB.
- 20 territories drawn by Natural Earth are not in `src/lib/geo/countries.ts` (listed in `worldData.test.ts`): they
  stay grey and cannot be tapped. Gibraltar (`GI`) is in the table but not in the 1:50m data.
- Borders are Natural Earth's de facto ones, including for disputed areas.
