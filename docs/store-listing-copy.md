### EN

```
Mistwalk turns an ordinary walk, run, or drive into map exploration.

The world map starts covered in fog. The moment you physically travel through an area — on foot, by bike, or by car — the fog clears there permanently, and that part of the map stays revealed. The more you move, the more of the map you uncover.

WHAT MISTWALK DOES
• Reveals the map in real time as you move — even while the app is in the background
• Tracks distance travelled, and countries and cities visited
• Surfaces nearby points of interest and notifies you when you're close to one you haven't found yet
• Sends a weekly summary of distance covered and new area revealed
• Works offline: download a map area in advance and keep revealing fog without a connection

SOCIAL FEATURES (optional)
• Follow friends and see their progress in an activity feed
• Compare progress on a shared leaderboard
• Make your profile private at any time

PRIVACY ON YOUR TERMS
Location is used only to power the app's core mechanic. We don't sell your data or use advertising SDKs. You can delete your account and all your data in one step, right in the app.

Mistwalk isn't a social network or a leaderboard-chasing game. It's a way to see how much of the world you've actually covered — and how much is still waiting.
```
